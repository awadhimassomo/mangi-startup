Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes — you can upgrade from Solo to Team at any time. Your data is always preserved.
What happens if I exceed my seat limit?
You'll be prompted to upgrade before adding a new team member. Existing members are never removed.
What is the Opportunity Board?
The Mangi team curates and posts funding opportunities — grants, competitions, accelerators — directly into your dashboard. Both plans get full access.
Do you offer discounts for NGOs or accelerators managing multiple startups?
Yes — contact us at hello@mangi.co for custom pricing.
